# MiniMines brand — extracted from the Mother PPT

Source: `MiniMInes Intro PPT - Mother PPT.pptx` (39 slides, 358 media files).
Extracted 23 Aug 2026. Values below are **measured, not guessed** — the method is noted for
each so you can correct anything that reads wrong.

## Logo

`brand/logo/image1.png` — the slide-master logo. Stacked wordmark **MINI / MINES** with the
double-O of MINI drawn as battery terminals (`+` and `−`), tagline **EXTRACTING WHAT MATTERS**.

Ink colour sampled pixel-by-pixel from the artwork itself: **`#005D7F`** (2657 px of a
single flat value — so this is exact, not an approximation).

Still needed: a vector (SVG or EPS) version, a single-colour and a reversed/white version for
dark grounds, and the clear-space rule. The PNG is 164×123 and will not hold up as a header
logo at high DPI.

The PPT theme block itself is untouched stock Office (Calibri, `#5B9BD5`…), so it carries no
brand information and is ignored.

| Albert Sans Light, Poppins, Gill Sans MT | 163 combined | Occasional |

Roboto and Roboto Condensed are both on Google Fonts, so they can be used in the console at
no cost and with no licensing question. Roboto Condensed is a natural fit for the dense data
columns (employee codes, item codes, token claims) and echoes the condensed feel of the
wordmark.

## What this gives the console

A palette that is genuinely MiniMines rather than invented: **petrol blue as primary, deep
navy for grounds and headings, orange as the single action/attention colour, cyan as the
light accent, and cool blue-tinted neutrals** — with Roboto throughout.

The placeholder palette used in the first prototype (nickel-sulfate teal, cobalt rose,
graphite) is now retired.
